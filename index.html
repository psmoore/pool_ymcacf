One question, fourteen websites

“Where can I swim laps before work on Tuesday?”

Today, answering that on ymcacf.org means:

  1. Open the Locations page and pick a Y.
  2. Find pool hours in that Y's hours list. They are pool hours, not lap swim hours.
  3. Open that Y's Family Center Schedule. It covers group exercise, not lap swim, and the schedule loads only when you scroll to it.
  4. Go back and do it again, up to thirteen more times.

Swim lessons are in a separate finder that searches one program at a time. Swim team practice times aren't published at all: both team pages ask families to fill out an interest form to see them.

On this calendar it's one look: pick Tuesday, choose Lap Swim, and every Y that's open at 6am is on the screen, with its lessons, classes and team practices beside it. The Y's own staff keep it current in an AI chat, and every page on the site updates at once.

Questions for the aquatics team

Everything on these calendars comes from what the Y publishes. These are the places where two of its own sources disagree, where a schedule isn't published, or where we had to make a call.

  • OursPool hours shown as lap swim
    Twelve location pages give pool hours, not lap swim times, and none say which lanes are held for laps while lessons and classes run. We show the pool hours as lap swim in a “Lap Lanes” part of each pool, and lessons and classes in a “Program Area” beside it. Roper and Winter Park publish lap swim itself, and those times are used as given.
  • GapSwim team practice times
    The Developmental team lists 11 locations and the USA Swim team 5, and both pages say to fill out an interest form “to see specific days and times for each location.” Only Winter Park and Dr. P. Phillips put practices on their class schedules, so only those two are on the swim team calendar.
  • ConflictWinter Park: lap swim and swim team, 4–7pm
    Winter Park's schedule has open lap swim until 7pm Monday through Thursday and swim team practice 4–7pm Tuesday through Thursday, both in the Lap Pool. How many lanes stay open for lap swimmers? Say, and the calendar shows it lane by lane.
  • ConflictDr. P. Phillips Developmental practice at 7:30am?
    The class schedule lists Developmental Swim Team Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 7:30–8:30am, through December 1. A school-day morning practice is unusual; is it 7:30pm?
  • ConflictRoper: two sets of lap swim hours
    The Roper location page gives pool hours Monday–Thursday 7–12:30 and 4–7:30. Its class schedule lists lap swim Monday, Wednesday and Friday mornings and 5–7:30pm Monday–Thursday. We used the class schedule; are Tuesday and Thursday mornings open?
  • ConflictLessons after the pool closes
    Leonard & Marjorie Williams lists its pool open until 7pm, and Tuesday Teen/Adult lessons at 7:00–7:30pm.
  • GapSession dates that don't add up
    At Osceola, Sunday School Age Stage 5 is a 4-lesson session listed as ending Wednesday, November 18, and Sunday Teen/Adult ends November 1, which is three Sundays. We showed both as four Sundays from October 18.
  • GapSmaller things
    Osceola's Friday lap swim reads “3:00 am – 5:45pm” (shown as 3pm). Frank DeLuca's Thursday and Friday Water Fitness are listed in Studio A (shown in the pool). Osceola and Wayne Densch have no classes on their class schedules at all.
