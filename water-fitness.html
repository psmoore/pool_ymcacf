Water Fitness

12 Ys · weekday mornings · included with membership

Aqua Zumba, shallow and deep water fitness, water tai chi and more, from every Y's class schedule on one calendar. Pick a day.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →  —  Almost every class is between 9 and noon on a weekday.

Fourteen class schedules, one pool of classes

Each Y's water fitness is in its own class schedule, among the cycling, BODYPUMP and racquetball court bookings, in a strip of a few rows you scroll inside the page.

Here are only the classes in the water, every Y at once. A member between Oviedo and Winter Park can see that Oviedo has Shallow Water Fitness at 9:15 every weekday and Winter Park has Aqua Deep at 9:45.
