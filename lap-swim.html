Lap Swim

14 Ys · from 5am Downtown to 9:30pm

Lap swim at every YMCA of Central Florida pool, included with membership. Pick a day; each row is one pool.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →  —  Tap a block to see the pool and the source of its hours.

Pool hours are not lap swim hours

Every location page lists when its pool is open. A swimmer who shows up at 9:30 on a weekday at Oviedo finds Shallow Water Fitness in the water; at Winter Park after 4pm on a Tuesday, swim team practice shares the lap pool.

On the calendar, lap swim stops where the Y's own schedule says something else is in the water. With the number of lanes each program takes, it would say how many lanes are left too, the way it already does for pools like Penobscot Bay YMCA.
