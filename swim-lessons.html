Group Swim Lessons

Fall sessions from October 17 · 11 Ys · ages 6 months to adult

Four-week group lesson sessions, $55 for members and $95 for non-members. Pick a day, and use the Practice Groups menu to show one level: Swim Starters (6 months to 3 years), Preschool (3–5), School Age (6–12) or Teen & Adult.

Sessions start October 17 The calendar opens on today, before the fall sessions begin. Tap the date at the top, then › to October and pick Saturday the 17th.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →  —  Each block lists the levels at that time and the spots still open in each.

“What's open for my four-year-old on Saturday?”

The Y's lesson finder can answer this, but for one lesson type at a time, starting from a zip code, as a list of cards. Comparing Preschool at three nearby Ys means three searches and a notepad.

Here, choose Preschool and Saturday, October 17: every Y offering it is on one screen, in time order, with the open spots in each class. It's the same data the finder uses, 424 sessions, laid out as a schedule.
