Swim Teams

Developmental at 11 Ys · USA Swimming at 5

The Developmental Swim Team is the step after swim lessons, with optional meets. The USA Swim Team practices five days a week and competes. Ages 6–17.

Live from Pool Relay · open the full calendar →  —  Only Winter Park and Dr. P. Phillips publish practice times today.

The schedule behind the interest form

Both swim team pages list their locations, then say: “Fill out the interest form below to see specific days and times for each location.” Winter Park and Dr. P. Phillips put their practices on their class schedules, so those two are here. For the other locations, a family has to give their contact details and wait to find out when practice is.

With each team's groups and times entered once, this calendar shows every location's practices side by side. Parents compare before they call, coaches see the lanes they hold, and lap swimmers see which lanes are left.
